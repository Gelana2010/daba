01 / ABOUT

Research grounded in Earth science.

I use Earth-observation data and geospatial methods to investigate geological systems and environmental change.

I am a researcher in the Remote Sensing Department at the Ethiopian Space Science and Geospatial Institute. My academic background brings together a BSc in Geology and an MSc in Remote Sensing, supporting an interdisciplinary approach to geospatial research.

My current work includes remote-sensing assessment of hydrothermally altered areas in the Main Ethiopian Rift and Earth-observation approaches for monitoring rangeland conditions and land-cover dynamics.

I am interested in collaborative research that integrates satellite imagery, field observations, GIS, and machine learning to produce evidence that supports scientific understanding and practical decision-making.
